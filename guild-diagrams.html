🌿 Guild Design Principles

7 functional roles: N-fixers, Dynamic accumulators, Mulchers, Pollinators, Pest repellents, Ground covers, Yield
3 concentric zones: Inner (0-60cm — shallow bulbs, bare mulch), Mid (drip line — main N-fixers, accumulators), Outer (edges — shrubs, transitional)
Multi-function: Every plant fills 2+ roles. Comfrey = accumulator + mulcher + insectary.
Site-adapted: Zone 8b-9a, 300m, silty-sand, Mediterranean drought.
