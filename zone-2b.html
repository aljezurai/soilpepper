🍎 Pomegranate — Punica granatum

🍎 Yield🔥 Fire-resist🐝 Pollinator

Soil benefit: Deep roots stabilize slope. Moderate leaf litter. Tolerates poor soil. Fire-resistant wood — excellent for SE firebreak zone.

Varieties: 'Dente di Cavallo' (Italian), 'Wonderful', 'Salavatski'. Spacing: 4m × 4m. Water: 💧 Low — dry-farmed after Y2. Frost: Hardy to -10°C. 🔥 Fire: High-moisture fruit + wood = good fire resistance.

🍒 Jujube — Ziziphus jujuba

🍎 Yield🐝 Pollinator🔥 Fire-resist

Soil benefit: Deep taproot breaks subsoil. Drought-adapted — minimal competition. Fixes slopes. Produces abundant leaf litter.

Varieties: 'Tibetan', 'Li', 'Lang'. Spacing: 4m × 4m. Water: 💧 Low — extremely drought-tolerant. Frost: Hardy to -15°C. 🔥 Fire: High-moisture wood. Excellent for firebreak zone.

🫐 Goji berry — Lycium barbarum

🍎 Yield🐝 Pollinator

Soil benefit: Deep roots. Tolerates poor, alkaline soil. Moderate leaf litter. Low competition — good under canopy trees.

Varieties: 'Sweet Lifeberry', 'Crimson Star'. Spacing: 2m apart. Water: 💧💧 Moderate — some irrigation for fruiting. Frost: Hardy to -15°C.

🫐 Elderberry — Sambucus nigra

🍎 Yield🐝 Pollinator🌿 Mulcher

Soil benefit: Fast-growing biomass producer. Chop-and-drop mulch. Attracts birds + pollinators. Shallow roots — good for swale edges.

Varieties: 'Sambu', 'Haschberg'. Spacing: 2.5m apart. Water: 💧💧 Moderate — needs some summer water. Frost: Hardy to -20°C.

🫐 Blackberry — Rubus spp.

🍎 Yield🐝 Pollinator🚫 Pest repel

Soil benefit: Dense root system stabilizes soil. Biennial canes add organic matter. Good for slope edges. Attracts birds and beneficial insects.

Varieties: 'Loch Ness' (thornless), 'Chester' (thornless). Spacing: 2m apart. Water: 💧💧 Moderate — more with summer watering. Frost: Hardy to -15°C. Strongly suited to Tuscany.

🫐 Raspberry ⚠️ — Rubus idaeus

🍎 Yield🐝 Pollinator🌱 Ground

Soil benefit: Dense root system. Moderate biomass. Needs heavy mulch. ⚠️ Marginal at this site: needs afternoon shade + consistent drip irrigation.

Varieties: 'Heritage', 'Autumn Bliss' (primocane = fruit on new wood, less frost risk). Spacing: 1m apart, 6-12 canes. Water: 💧💧💧 High — drip non-negotiable. Frost: Canes hardy to -25°C.

🫐 Red currant ⚠️ — Ribes rubrum

🍎 Yield🐝 Pollinator

Soil benefit: Low biomass. Needs annual compost. ⚠️ Marginal: struggles in Mediterranean heat. Needs afternoon shade + drip.

Varieties: 'Jonkheer van Tets', 'Rovada'. Spacing: 1.5m apart, 2-3 bushes only. Water: 💧💧💧 High — constant moisture essential. Frost: Hardy to -30°C.

🔄 Sea buckthorn — Hippophae rhamnoides

🔄 N-fixer🍎 Yield🚫 Windbreak

Soil benefit: N-fixer — enriches soil for surrounding plants. Deep root system stabilizes slopes. Excellent windbreak. Edible berries high in vitamin C. Thorns = natural barrier.

Spacing: 2m apart in hedge. Water: 💧 Low — extremely drought-tolerant. Frost: Hardy to -25°C. Male + female plants needed for fruit.

🔄 Goumi — Elaeagnus multiflora

🔄 N-fixer🐝 Pollinator🍎 Yield

Soil benefit: N-fixer — root nodules enrich soil. Edible berries. Attracts pollinators. Dense shrub form. Non-invasive. Tolerates dry conditions.

Spacing: 2m apart. Water: 💧💧 Moderate — some irrigation for fruiting. Frost: Hardy to -15°C. Excellent companion for Zone 2b edges.

🌿 Comfrey — Symphytum officinale

⛏️ Accumulator🌿 Mulcher🐝 Pollinator

Soil benefit: KEY plant. Deep taproot mines minerals from subsoil. Chop-and-drop 3-5x/year = green manure. Leaves = potassium-rich mulch. Accumulator of K, P, Ca, Mg. Attracts bees (purple flowers).

Varieties: Bocking 14 (sterile — won't self-seed). Spacing: 1m apart. Water: 💧💧 Moderate — needs some water in summer for best growth. Frost: Dies back in winter, regrows. Hardy to -20°C.

🌼 Yarrow — Achillea millefolium

⛏️ Accumulator🐝 Pollinator🌿 Mulcher

Soil benefit: Deep taproot mines K, Ca, P, Cu. Increases essential oil content in nearby herbs (companion effect). Excellent pollinator attractor. Medicinal. Drought-tolerant.

Spacing: 0.5m apart, self-seeds. Water: 💧 Low — drought-tolerant. Frost: Hardy to -25°C.

🌸 Borage — Borago officinalis

🐝 Pollinator⛏️ Accumulator🍎 Edible

Soil benefit: Deep taproot mines minerals. Excellent bee forage (blue flowers = bee magnet). Self-seeds (permanent patch). Edible flowers and leaves. Repels tomato hornworm. Good chop-and-drop biomass.

Spacing: 0.4m apart. Water: 💧 Low — self-seeds and persists. Frost: Annual — self-seeds reliably.

🌱 Thyme — Thymus vulgaris

🌱 Ground cover🚫 Pest repel🐝 Pollinator

Soil benefit: Excellent ground cover — prevents erosion. Low-growing mat suppresses weeds. Releases fragrance when walked on. Attracts bees. Pest-repellent. Drought-tolerant.

Varieties: Creeping thyme for paths, upright for kitchen. Spacing: 0.3m apart for full coverage. Water: 💧 Low — never irrigate after establishment. 🔥 Fire: Good — low fuel volume.

🌱 White clover — Trifolium repens

🔄 N-fixer🌱 Ground cover🐝 Pollinator

Soil benefit: N-fixer — converts atmospheric N to plant-available form. Living mulch — suppresses weeds, retains moisture. Bee forage. Chicken forage. Tolerates foot traffic.

Varieties: 'Ladino', 'Dutch White'. Spacing: Scatter seed. Water: 💧 Low — self-seeds in Mediterranean. Frost: Hardy to -20°C.

🍇 Grape (table) — Vitis vinifera

🍎 Yield🐝 Pollinator

Soil benefit: Deep root system (3m+) breaks up subsoil. Low nutrient competition with shallow-rooted plants. Mycorrhizal. Climbs up canopy trees = vertical stacking.

Varieties: 'Italia' (table), 'Moscato' (table/wine). Spacing: One vine per canopy tree, climbing trunk + branches. Water: 💧 Low — dry-farmed after Y1. Frost: Dormant to -15°C.
