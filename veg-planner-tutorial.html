2🖐️
Drag plants into beds

Drag from the palette onto a bed. The planner watches for companion conflicts and warns you.

🥕 Drag plants
🔄Move vegetables around — drag any plant already in a bed to another bed (or another spot) to rearrange. Hover/tap a plant and use the ✕ to remove it. Your layout stays in sync everywhere.
🟰10m vs 20m beds — each slot is a 10m bed. Tap the join toggle on a row to merge two 10m beds into one 20m bed, or split it back. Handy for long rows like beans or squash.
3🌱
Know what to sow now

Use Sow this month to see exactly what's ready to plant in the current month.

🌱 Sow this month📅 Sow / harvest
🗓️Sow this month highlights the plants whose sowing window is open right now — nothing to sow? It says so, so you're never guessing.
📐Spacing & distance in the popup — tap any plant to open its card: it shows the sowing pattern (square / row / hill), exact seed spacing, row distance, seed depth, thinning and how many plants fit one 7.5 m² slot.
