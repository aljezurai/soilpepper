🌶️ SOILPEPPER — Permaculture Design Toolkit

Plan a permaculture site from blank plot to garden beds — no account, no server, no data leaving your device. Desktop tools for designing, mobile tools for the field. 256 species with guild profiles, companion scoring and sowing specs. Built on Aranya's step-by-step method.

🔒 Privacy by design: everything runs in your browser. Your site plans, water calculations and garden layouts are stored in your browser's localStorage — they never touch a server. Export a field view to take plans to your garden, phone or printer.
